# OFFICIAL DATA SOURCE REGISTRY
Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This document defines every approved source of information used by Janavani.

Only approved sources may populate the platform database.

Every stored value must be traceable to an approved source.

=

---

# AI Rule

Artificial Intelligence may explain data.

Artificial Intelligence may compare data.

Artificial Intelligence may summarise data.

Artificial Intelligence shall never replace the original source.

The original source always remains authoritative.

---

# Data Refresh Policy

Election Data

Refresh only after official publication.

Legislative Data

Daily during sessions.

Government Orders

Daily.

Fund Utilisation

Weekly.

Official Profiles

Weekly.

Affidavits

After every election.

RTI Responses

When received.
