# JANAVANI CONSTITUTION
Version: 1.0
Status: Draft
Project: Janavani Intelligence Platform

---

# 1. Vision

Janavani is an open, evidence-based Civic Intelligence Platform designed to help citizens, researchers, journalists, civil society organisations, and public institutions understand, verify, analyse, and improve governance using trusted public information.

Janavani is not a political platform.

Janavani is not a social media platform.

Janavani is not a news platform.

Janavani is a Public Intelligence Platform.

---

# 2. Mission

To build India's most trusted public governance intelligence platform by combining:

• Official Government Data
• Constitutional Principles
• Public Records
• Artificial Intelligence
• Knowledge Graphs
• Evidence-based Analysis

into one transparent and explainable system.

---

# 3. First Principle

Every conclusion must be supported by evidence.

Evidence always comes before opinion.

---

# 4. Core Values

• Truthfulness
• Transparency
• Explainability
• Privacy
• Accountability
• Constitutional Governance
• Open Standards
• Human-Centred Design

---

# 5. What Janavani Is

Janavani is an adaptive Civic Intelligence Platform.

The platform helps citizens understand public governance using verified public information.

---

# 6. What Janavani Is NOT

Janavani is NOT:

• a political party
• a propaganda platform
• a news organisation
• a social media network
• a lobbying organisation

Janavani provides evidence.

Citizens form opinions.

---

# 7. Version 1 Scope

The first release focuses only on Kerala.

Included:

• 20 Members of Parliament (MP)
• 140 Members of Legislative Assembly (MLA)

Nothing else.

Version 1 deliberately excludes every other feature.

---

# 8. Evidence Policy

Every public fact displayed by Janavani must include:

• Source
• Date Retrieved
• Last Updated
• Verification Status

No unsupported claims are permitted.

---

# 9. AI Policy

Artificial Intelligence may:

• Summarise
• Compare
• Translate
• Explain
• Search
• Recommend

Artificial Intelligence must never invent facts.

Every AI response must be traceable to evidence whenever factual claims are made.

---

# 10. Privacy Policy

Janavani stores only information that is publicly available or voluntarily provided by users.

Private life information is outside the scope of the platform.

---

# 11. Public Representative Policy

Profiles include only professional public information.

Examples:

• Office Held
• Constituency
• Legislative Performance
• Committee Membership
• Election Affidavits
• Fund Utilisation
• Official Social Media
• Official Contact Information

The platform excludes:

• Family information
• Religion
• Caste
• Personal Address
• Personal Phone Numbers
• Personal Email
• Private Photographs
• Rumours

---

# 12. Long-term Vision

Janavani will evolve into a modular Civic Intelligence Platform supporting:

• Governance Intelligence
• Department Intelligence
• Scheme Intelligence
• Budget Intelligence
• RTI Intelligence
• Complaint Intelligence
• Community Intelligence

Each module operates independently while sharing a common architecture.

---
